# Coop IT-Strategy Agent Project — Project Brief

## Purpose

This project supports a written IT-strategy exam paper about Coop Denmark, Coop App and Lobyco.

The goal is not to produce a finished paper immediately. The goal is to build a controlled, human-in-the-loop workflow where agents create options, the user chooses, decisions are logged, and the final paper remains aligned with the official case assignment.

The official assignment requires:

1. an analysis of Coop's strategic situation in 2026, and
2. 2-5 relevant strategic recommendations based on that analysis.

The advice must prepare one selected member of Coop's executive management to participate in an expected executive discussion about the future of Coop App and Lobyco.

North star:

> Analyse Coop's strategic situation first; on that basis, develop 2-5 relevant strategic recommendations in continuation of the analysis.

## Case Focus

The case concerns Coop's digital strategy, especially:

- Coop App
- Lobyco
- customer loyalty
- retail media
- app-based shopping experiences
- closure of coop.dk
- Coop's financial pressure
- the relationship between digital initiatives and physical stores
- whether Coop should strengthen, reduce, sell, differentiate or rethink its app/Lobyco strategy

## Official Case Tension

After the 2025 annual report, members of Coop's executive management expect CEO Thor Skov Jorgensen to discuss whether Coop should continue being market-leading on Coop App when Coop's bricks-and-mortar stores across Denmark are central to Coop's brand.

The expected discussion includes whether Coop should:

- reduce investment and continued development of the app,
- possibly sell or divest Lobyco,
- strengthen app investment further,
- accept the opportunity cost this creates for other group investments,
- "go deep" and develop significantly different apps for different chains,
- pursue other possible options.

## Working Strategic Tension

Coop App and Lobyco may represent important digital capabilities, but their strategic value must be assessed against Coop's current reality:

- Coop is financially pressured.
- Coop is dependent on physical stores after closing coop.dk.
- Competitors may compete through price, formats, logistics and simplicity, not only through digital tools.
- Several app-related sources are marketing or supplier sources.
- App adoption and engagement do not automatically prove profitability.

## Working Thesis

Coop should not treat Coop App as a separate digital prestige project. The app and Lobyco should be treated as strategic assets only if they strengthen Coop's core business: physical stores, customer loyalty, supplier value, store traffic, differentiated chain positioning and financial turnaround.

This thesis is provisional and must remain open until the user chooses a strategic stance on Coop App/Lobyco investment.

## Exam Constraints

- The paper should be 5-25 pages.
- The assignment material states that the paper may be written in Danish or English.
- User decision for this project: write drafts and LLM outputs in Norwegian unless later changed. Before final hand-in, confirm whether Norwegian is accepted by the course/exam rules.
- It must analyse a real company where digital opportunities are central.
- It must apply 1-2 selected course perspectives.
- The selected perspectives can be theories, concepts or selected parts of broader course perspectives.
- If only parts of a perspective are used, the selection must be justified and transparent.
- It must formulate strategic advice to one of the case-specified actors.
- It should prioritize depth over breadth.
- It should contain one overall convincing argument.
- It should use a traditional structure: case, chosen perspectives, analytical results and strategic recommendations.

## Human-In-The-Loop Rule

The workflow must follow this sequence:

```text
Generate options -> explain pros/cons -> recommend -> user chooses -> log decision -> continue
```

No final report should be written before the major decision gates are completed.

The project must describe all important choices and assumptions.
