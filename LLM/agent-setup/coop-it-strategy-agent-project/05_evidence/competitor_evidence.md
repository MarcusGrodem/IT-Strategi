## Evidence Questions For Competitor Agent

1. Are Coop's competitors winning mainly through digital tools, or through price, logistics, formats and simplicity?
2. Are loyalty apps differentiating factors or expected baseline tools?
3. Does competitor evidence support a chain-specific Coop App strategy?
4. Does competitor evidence support a stronger retail media strategy?
5. Does online grocery evidence make coop.dk closure look like strategic weakness or realistic discipline?

## Use In Final Paper

Competitor evidence should be used selectively. The exam paper should not become a broad market report.

Use competitor evidence only when it strengthens one of these analytical points:

- Coop App must create store-level value.
- Coop must differentiate app logic by chain.
- Coop cannot rely on digital loyalty if price perception is weak.
- Lobyco/retail media may be valuable if supplier-funded value is credible.
- Coop's closure of coop.dk changes how digital strategy should be evaluated.

## Most Important Imported Takeaway

The strongest imported insight is:

> Competitors appear strongest when their digital tools support their core business model, customer promise and operational setup. Salling links apps to store flow and scale; Lidl links Lidl Plus to price/value; REMA uses narrow in-store digital utility around a clear discount model; Dagrofa/MENY link digital/member tools to food and local identity; Nemlig shows that online grocery requires a dedicated logistics model and still has difficult economics.

This supports a source-critical Coop argument: Coop App reach is relevant, but its strategic value depends on fit with physical stores, chain differentiation and economics.
