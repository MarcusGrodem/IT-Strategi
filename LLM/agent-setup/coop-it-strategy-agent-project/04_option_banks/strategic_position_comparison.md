## Evidence Tests Before Choosing

| Test | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Is there strong evidence that app use affects store behaviour or customer value? | Strengthen, go deep or hybrid become stronger | Reduce/simplify positions become stronger |
| Is financial pressure central and well documented? | Hybrid, reduce or CEO angle become stronger | Strengthen app becomes easier |
| Are app success claims mostly marketing claims? | Hybrid/source-critical positions become stronger | Strengthen app may be defensible |
| Do Coop chains have clearly different strategic needs? | Go deep by chain becomes stronger | One shared app strategy may be more defensible |
| Is Lobyco valuable beyond Coop? | Controlled capability/platform angle becomes stronger | Lobyco should be treated mainly as Coop-support infrastructure |
| Does competitor evidence show digital tools work best when fitted to business models? | Go deep, hybrid and selective simplification become stronger | Broad app-strengthening becomes easier |
