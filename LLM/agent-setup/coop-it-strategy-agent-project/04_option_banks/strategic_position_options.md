# Strategic Stance Option Bank — Coop App / Lobyco

The official case asks whether Coop should reduce app investment, possibly sell Lobyco, strengthen the app, "go deep" with significantly different apps for different chains, or consider other options.

This file compares those strategic stances. No stance is chosen yet.

| Option | Strategic stance | What the paper would argue | Pros | Cons / Risks | Score | Recommended? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Reduce app investment | Coop should reduce investment and continued app development to prioritise bricks-and-mortar and financial recovery | Strongly addresses financial pressure and opportunity cost | May underuse Coop's existing app reach and digital capability; can seem anti-digital | 6 | No |
| 2 | Reduce app investment and sell/partly divest Lobyco | Coop should simplify focus and potentially release capital by divesting Lobyco | Bold and directly answers the case question | High evidence burden; may lose strategic capability and future retail media/customer-data value | 5 | No |
| 3 | Strengthen app investment | Coop should continue or increase market-leading app ambition | Builds on app reach, Lobyco and digital customer relationship | Risk of relying on marketing claims; may ignore investment trade-offs and store economics | 6 | No |
| 4 | Go deep by chain | Coop should develop significantly different app experiences or modules for different chains | Directly matches case question; supported by competitor evidence that digital tools should fit chain model | More complexity and cost; requires governance and technical discipline | 9 | Possible |
| 5 | Hybrid governance | Coop should keep App/Lobyco, but tie further investment to store economics, chain-specific value and strict KPIs | Balanced, source-critical and fits both app potential and bricks-and-mortar reality | Must be made concrete to avoid sounding like compromise language | 10 | Possible |

## Current Interpretation

No choice is locked.

The current evidence most strongly supports comparing:

- Option 4: Go deep by chain.
- Option 5: Hybrid governance.
- Option 6: Selective app simplification.

Option 3, "strengthen app investment", needs stronger evidence that app engagement translates into store-level or financial value.

Option 2, "sell Lobyco", needs stronger evidence that Lobyco is a distraction or has greater sale value than strategic value.
