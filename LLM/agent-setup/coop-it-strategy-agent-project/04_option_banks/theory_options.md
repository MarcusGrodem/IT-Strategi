## Agent Recommendation

Choose Option 2: DVC Framework + Digital Business Model perspective.

This combination gives a focused but rich analysis:

- DVC can analyse what digital value the app claims to create for customers, relationships and relevance.
- Digital Business Model thinking can analyse whether Lobyco and retail media represent a broader strategic value logic or just a support function.

The limitation must be stated clearly: neither perspective proves profitability. The analysis must therefore be combined with source criticism, financial pressure and store-based strategic implications.
