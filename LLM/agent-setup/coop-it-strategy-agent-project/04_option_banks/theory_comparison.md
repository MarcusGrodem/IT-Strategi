## Theory Fit Questions

Before choosing theory, answer:

1. Is the central question about customer value?
   - DVC is likely necessary.

2. Is the central question about Lobyco/app as a broader value logic?
   - Digital Business Model perspective becomes useful.

3. Is retail media and supplier interaction central?
   - Platform/ecosystem perspective may be useful.

4. Is execution under financial pressure central?
   - Implementation perspective may be useful.

5. Is competitor positioning central?
   - Porter/positioning may be useful, but it risks broadening the paper.
