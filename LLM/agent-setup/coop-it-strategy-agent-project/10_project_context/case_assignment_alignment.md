# Case Assignment Alignment

This file records the corrected project strategy based on the official case description.

## Core Assignment Logic

The assignment has two main tasks:

1. Analyse Coop's strategic situation in 2026.
2. Based on that analysis, develop 2-5 relevant strategic recommendations.

The recommendations must prepare one selected member of Coop's executive management to participate in an expected executive discussion about the future of Coop App and Lobyco.

## North Star

Use this sentence as the controlling logic for every later agent:

> Jeres opgave er at I) analysere virksomhedens strategiske situation; og på denne baggrund II) udarbejde 2-5 relevante strategiske anbefalinger i forlængelse af jeres analyse.

This means:

- Recommendations must not come before analysis.
- Recommendations must follow directly from the strategic situation analysis.
- The analysis must be broad enough to explain Coop's situation, but focused enough to support app/Lobyco decisions.
- The final recommendations should be traceable back to evidence, source criticism and theory.

## Theory Requirement From Assignment

The recommendations must be based on 1-2 self-selected perspectives from the course.

The perspectives may be:

- theories,
- concepts,
- frameworks,
- selected parts of a broader perspective.

The project does not need to use an entire theory or framework if only some parts are relevant. However, this selection must be:

- well justified,
- transparent,
- connected to the case,
- used analytically,
- connected to the recommendations.

Practical rule:

```text
Selected perspective part -> why relevant -> how applied to Coop -> what it reveals -> limitation -> recommendation implication
```

This means the theory section should not be a general textbook explanation. It should explain why the selected part of the perspective helps analyse Coop's situation.

## Expected Executive Discussion

After the 2025 annual report, several members of Coop's executive management expect CEO Thor Skov Jorgensen to discuss:

- whether Coop should continue being market-leading on the customer-facing Coop App,
- whether this still makes sense when Coop's bricks-and-mortar stores across Denmark are central to the Coop brand,
- whether investment and continued app development should be reduced,
- whether Lobyco should possibly be sold,
- whether app investment should instead be strengthened,
- what stronger app investment would mean for other investment possibilities in the group,
- whether Coop should "go deep" and develop significantly different apps for the different chains,
- whether other options exist.

## Correct Strategic Frame

The paper should not be framed as a general digital transformation essay.

The correct frame is:

> How should Coop think strategically about future investment in Coop App and Lobyco, given Coop's 2026 strategic situation, financial pressure, physical-store brand core and different chain needs?

## Required Advisory Role

The paper must advise one of the following people:

1. Adm. direktor / CEO: Thor Skov Jorgensen
2. Direktør SuperBrugsen og Kvickly: Rikke Krause
3. Direktør Brugsen: Torben B. Andersen
4. Direktør 365discount: Michael Tilsted
5. Direktør for Kommunikation, Marketing og Digital: Martin Hasgard Olesen

The advice must enable the chosen person to participate in the expected executive discussion on a qualified basis.

This means the recommendation angle depends on the selected actor:

| Actor | What they need to be prepared for |
|---|---|
| Thor Skov Jorgensen | Group-level trade-off between app/Lobyco investment, possible divestment and group investment priorities |
| Rikke Krause | How Coop App/Lobyco should support SuperBrugsen and Kvickly's customer promise, physical-store experience and chain positioning |
| Torben B. Andersen | How app strategy should support Brugsen's local, member-owned and community/store identity |
| Michael Tilsted | Whether 365discount needs a simpler, price-focused and possibly distinct app logic |
| Martin Hasgard Olesen | How Coop should govern customer-facing digital, loyalty, retail media and communication across chains |

## Strategic Option Space From The Case

The assignment itself names several possible strategic paths. These must structure the project:

| Case option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Reduce app investment | Spend less on app development and possibly shift resources to stores/operations |
| Sell or partly divest Lobyco | Reduce complexity or free capital, but risk losing digital capability |
| Strengthen app investment | Continue or increase market-leading app ambition |
| Go deep by chain | Develop significantly different app experiences for different Coop chains |
| Other options | Hybrid governance, KPI discipline, retail media boundaries, selective feature reduction, chain-specific modules |

## Implication For Agent Setup

The agent workflow must be reorganised around the case's executive-discussion problem:

```text
Strategic situation analysis
        ->
Evaluate case option space
        ->
Compare implications for each possible advised executive
        ->
Use 1-2 course perspectives
        ->
Develop 2-5 recommendations for the selected executive
```

The open executive choice is still valid, but the option bank must use the exact five case actors.

The open strategic-position choice should be renamed or reframed as:

> Strategic stance on Coop App/Lobyco investment.

## Must Include In Final Paper

The final paper must explicitly state:

- selected executive,
- why that executive is selected,
- selected 1-2 course perspectives,
- why these perspectives are selected,
- assumptions made,
- source criticism,
- analysis of Coop's strategic situation,
- evaluation of the app/Lobyco option space,
- 2-5 recommendations that prepare the selected executive for the executive discussion.
