# Who we are
## Coop Trading is the largest private brand purchasing organization in the Nordics
Coop Trading is responsible for delivering high quality private brand products at the best price for the biggest cooperative retail companies in Sweden, Denmark, Norway and Finland.
Our main activity is sourcing of food and daily nearfood products, securing branded products at competitive prices, and developing and maintaining a varied assortment of competitive private brand products – Xtra, Coop, and Änglamark. Our products are sold in more than 4,000 stores across the Nordics. 
We develop products for approximately 13 million consumers who shop in our well-known and market-leading stores in Sweden, Denmark, Norway and Finland.
