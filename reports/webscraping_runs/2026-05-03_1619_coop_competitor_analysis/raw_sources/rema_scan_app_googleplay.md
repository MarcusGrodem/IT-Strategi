## About this app
_arrow_forward_
With REMA1000's SCAN SELV app, you can now pack while shopping and skip the queue when paying. Simple & fast!  
  
SCAN SELV can be used in all REMA1000 stores.  
  
When you are in the store:  
1. Find your store in the app  
2. Scan your items  
3. Pay with a single swipe  
4. Scan the QR-code in the app by the exit  
5. Take your products with you and enjoy the rest of your day  
  
You can also see and share all your SCAN SELV receipts.  
  
Create an account easily in the app – if you already have a REMA1000 account, it can be used on all REMA1000 platforms, including the SCAN SELV app.
