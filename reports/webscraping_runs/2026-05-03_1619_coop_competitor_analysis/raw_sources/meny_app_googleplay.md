## About this app
_arrow_forward_
With the MENY app you get access to member prices and food inspiration.   
  
Enjoy having the week's newspapers, games, recipes, shopping lists and much more, right at your fingertips.   
  
Download the app, link your payment card, and enjoy extra benefits and food inspiration - directly to you.   
  
See you in MENY!   
  
By registering, you consent to receiving marketing via email and push messages. Your consent involves the processing of your member information and cookie consent for targeted marketing in emails, the member app and on websites, cf. Dagrofa Privacy Policy.   
  
Use of member prices requires the connection of a valid payment card.
