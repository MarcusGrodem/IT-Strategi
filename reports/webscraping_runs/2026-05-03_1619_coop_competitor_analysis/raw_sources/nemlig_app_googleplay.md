## About this app
_arrow_forward_
Groceries and Meal Boxes - delivered right to the door at your convenience.  
With namely.com's app you can find all kinds of groceries at your fingertips. Search for great deals, add your favorite items to the basket or add items to your shopping list that can easily be added to the basket with a single click  
  
At namely.com you will find the country's largest selection of organic fruits and vegetables and Denmark's largest selection of organic meats. If you are looking for delicacies, then.com offers 25 different farm and specialty shops where you can explore everything from French cheeses to homemade chocolates.  
  
If you need inspiration for the evening meals, you will find over 2000 different recipes in our meal universe. If things are going even faster, you can also choose from our 6 delicious Meal Boxes that contain dinner dishes for the whole week - and if you prefer to combine your own favorites, you can choose Build-Yourself and put together your own Meal Box out of 24 different dishes.  
  
namely.com's app has won awards for its user-friendly design, and the app is among the best rated apps on the Danish App Store. Download the.com app today for an easier, more fun and more personal shopping experience.
