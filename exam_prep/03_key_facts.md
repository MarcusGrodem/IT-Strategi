## The "Lobyco question" — What You Know vs. Don't Know

**Known:** Lobyco is a wholly-owned subsidiary, operates app infrastructure, claims 1.8M users, has started selling platform to other retailers (potential modular producer).

**Not known from public sources:** Lobyco's cost structure, its external revenues, its book value, the degree of dependency between Coop App and Lobyco technology, what a buyer would pay.

**Conclusion:** Cannot recommend selling or keeping Lobyco without more data. Recommend governance clarification: Coop-first mandate, measurable KPIs.
