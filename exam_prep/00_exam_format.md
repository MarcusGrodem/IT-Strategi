# Exam Format — IT-Strategi CBS

## Structure (from Lektion 9 slides)

- **Group oral exam** — ~20 minutes per student including deliberation
- **Internal censor** (ingen ekstern)
- **Basis**: Your written paper + entire curriculum
- **3 parts:**
  1. 4–5 minute opening presentation on self-chosen topics from the paper
  2. Questions and discussion about the written paper
  3. Questions and discussion about topics from the rest of the curriculum
- **Individual overall grade** based on paper + oral performance

## Your Paper

- **Advised actor:** Martin Hasgard Olesen, Direktør for Kommunikation, Marketing og Digital
- **Case:** Coop App and Lobyco — future investment and governance
- **Theories:** DVC Framework + Digital Business Model perspective (Weill & Woerner)

- **5 recommendations**
- **Language:** Norwegian (confirm with CBS rules before final submission)

## What Examiners Assess

From the quality criteria (Rienecker & Jørgensen):
- Masters the course learning objectives
- Presents as an independent, authoritative voice
- Employs relevant theory from the course
- Has a clear focus and limitations
- Contains one, overall convincing argument
- Is critical in approach (discusses pros and cons)
- Can apply, discuss and reflect — not just present
- Contains "meta-communication" (what happens next, limitations)

## Learning Objectives (Course)

- Account for fundamental IT-strategic models and concepts
- Describe and explain relevant IT-strategies applicable in different organizational contexts
- Identify and reflect on relevant concepts/models to analyze IT challenges and opportunities
- **Apply** course concepts and models meaningfully and reflectively on cases

## Your Opening (4–5 min): Choose wisely

Pick 1–2 topics from your paper where you can show depth and defend yourself well. Good options:
- The choice of hybrid governance stance and why not the alternatives
- How DVC + Digital Business Model work together to analyse Coop
- The source criticism argument (why you treat Lobyco/Playable claims carefully)
- KPI hierarchy recommendation (what "success" should mean for Coop App)
